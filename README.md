# Responsible AI at Oxlume

How the AI in [Oxlume](https://oxlume.com) is designed: what it does, what it does not do and the rules we follow.

> **Stage:** Oxlume is in private beta. This document describes how the product is designed today and what we are building towards. Our legal texts are reviewed by counsel before commercial launch; until then they are marked as drafts.

## What the AI does

Oxlume turns the photos already on a business owner's phone into short videos for Instagram, TikTok and Google, every week. Several models work together on every post:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1. Ingest | Vision models tag every photo: dish, mood, light, people, text. The menu or price list becomes structured prices. |
| 2. Plan | A planning agent picks the week's posts from the calendar, weather, local events and performance history, and writes the reasons in plain language. |
| 3. Compose | A composer chooses frames, builds motion, sets text and music, and renders a 9:16 video. |
| 4. Review | A reviewer checks prices, spelling, blur and platform policy, and adds the AI label. |
| 5. Learn | Views, saves and the owner's edits feed the next plan. |

By default the AI only moves, crops, times and captions what the owner shot. An optional creative mode can add generated backgrounds or product scenes; it is off by default and labelled when on.

## What the AI does not do

- **Publish on its own.** Nothing goes live without the owner's tap, unless the owner switches on autopilot for a specific format.
- **Hide that it is AI.** Any video edited or generated by AI carries a visible label and platform metadata. Real photos with motion and text are still marked as AI-edited.
- **Log in as you.** It connects only through the official Meta, Google and TikTok developer programmes. It never asks for passwords and never runs a bot on an account.
- **Generate people.** Creative mode cannot generate people and never puts words in anyone's mouth.
- **Invent claims.** The reviewer blocks invented reviews, invented awards, health claims and prices that don't match the menu.
- **Share your images.** A business's images never appear in anyone else's video.

## Who decides

The owner has the last word. One tap approves a video, one sentence changes it. The AI proposes, the owner decides.

- Autopilot exists, but it is opt-in per format and can be switched off in one tap from any notification.
- Every decision in the weekly plan is editable, and the planning agent remembers the owner's corrections.

## Data and consent

- **Your photos, your model.** A business's photos and results train the model of that business only. Anonymised performance patterns improve the planner for everyone.
- **People in photos.** There is a consent checkbox per photo with faces, and the reviewer flags unconsented faces.
- **Storage.** Data lives in EU data centres. Media is stored encrypted and access is logged.
- **Deletion and export.** When accounts are disconnected, media is deleted within 30 days; export is available before that.
- **Model providers.** Model calls run without retention at the provider, under enterprise terms.
- **Account access.** OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest, scoped to publishing only and revocable from the app.

## Rules we are building for

| Requirement | In Oxlume |
|---|---|
| EU AI Act, Article 50: disclose AI-generated content | Visible label plus platform metadata on every AI-edited post, on by default, cannot be disabled |
| GDPR: lawful basis, access, deletion | Contract as basis, export and delete from the app, EU hosting, DPA for business customers |
| Platform terms (Meta, TikTok, Google) | Official APIs, reviewed permissions, rate limits respected, no automation outside the APIs |
| Image rights of people in your photos | Consent checkbox per photo with faces; the reviewer flags unconsented faces |

More detail: [oxlume.com/trust.html](https://oxlume.com/trust.html)
